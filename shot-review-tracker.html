Loading a CSV

Click Load review CSV and pick an export (an 8-column shot list: Shot ID, Shot Name, timecodes, Character, Clip Status, Review Status). Everything fills in instantly and is remembered next time you open this page.

Or click Load sample data to see it populated with mock shots.

The pages

All Shots — everything in the loaded CSV, searchable.

Needs Refinement — shots with Review Status = NEEDS_REFINEMENT.

Set to Plate — rejected shots + not-started clips, auto-routed.

Sent to VFX — manual-only decisions, shown here for completeness.

Good to know

• A shot can have two characters with different statuses — each is its own row.
• Search boxes filter each table live.
• Data stays in your browser (localStorage) — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
